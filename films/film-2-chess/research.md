## Wording choices (to stay accurate)
- Hook says "the piece standing next to the king" (it was a minister/vizier for most of those years, a queen only from ~1000).
- Persian story and wheat story are told as stories ("According to a book...", "this is a legend").
- Louis IX: an ordinance against gambling that caught chess, not "Louis banned chess" (sources disagree on how total it was).
- Isabella: told as a disputed theory.
- 600 million: "an estimated".
