# Facts on Demand - the recurring cast

Three main characters appear in every film and every era. Everyone else is an NPC
(crowds, soldiers, spectators): drawn freely, in muted colours, so the cast stands out.

| Slot | Folder | Build | Hair | Scarf | Role / identity details |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | main-character/ | medium | dark brown, messy | ORANGE scarf | the curious one |
| 2 | (to be locked) | same as #1 | natural ginger, short and tousled | ORANGE scarf | the skeptic: round black glasses, grumpy look |
| 3 | (to be locked) | same as #1 | black, in a bun | ORANGE scarf | the clever one: freckles, confident smile |

Rules
- The ORANGE scarf is the team colour (and the channel's accent): all three always wear it.
  Tell them apart by hair, glasses (#2), bun and freckles (#3) and expression.
- ALL THREE share the body of #1: short compact build, thin BLACK single-line stick arms and legs,
  round white mitten hands, no ears, no fingers. They differ by hair, face details and colour.
- Each character has ONE locked master sheet. Never redraw a character from text.
- Era versions are made FROM the master sheet: only clothes, headwear and facial hair change.
- Identity across eras = hair colour, glasses / bun / freckles, the orange scarf and expression.
- Every new sheet is checked against character.json before it is used.
